## Design

One graphite theme from the mark (#17212b tile, #f5f6f8 stroke, #a7c8ff nodes),
amber for attention, green for passing, dashed grey for unknown. Mona Sans
(variable width) for text and Martian Mono for data labels, both OFL, self-hosted
through `next/font`. Metric-matched local fallbacks prevent reflow on font swap.

- Hero: the mark's open stroke drawn once as a five-step timeline beside the
  headline (desktop only).
- The model: one certificate signal followed through Observe, Relate, Recall,
  Plan, Brief. Packets are Observatory-style dots with two trailing points and
  an arrival ring; no permanent arrows. Finite sequence with Pause/Play/Replay,
  selectable steps and records, and an inspector with relations and changes.
- Views: the same seven records re-arranged per lens, with connector lines
  that re-route between lenses.
- Signals: a check worker probing resources grouped by project; the unchecked
  resource never receives a dot. Pause control; guide shown only on inspection.
- Agents: a paper brief with objective, cited evidence, May / May not and the
  review point, beside "Available now" and "In development".

Avoided: purple gradients, gradient text, blurred orbs, glass dashboards, grid
backdrops, pill CTAs, italic accents, numbered chapters, fake terminals or chats,
testimonials, logo walls, pricing, signup, waitlist and analytics.

## Remaining work

The apex change needs two steps the implementing session could not complete:

1. The Caddy routing lives in `deploy/commandry-rolling.py`, an enrolled
   control file. Changing it holds automatic promotion until the owner reviews
   the controls and re-enrolls their fingerprint as root on the VPS.
2. Reading the live Caddy and Tunnel configuration on the VPS was refused by
   the session's permission policy, so the live baseline could not be captured
   by the agent.

The cutover change (private app `/app` + `/login`, Caddy route split, tests)
is prepared as a pull request for owner review. Until it is installed,
`commandry.site/` keeps serving the private application exactly as before.

## Rollback

- Public site content: `vercel rollback <previous-deployment-url> --scope iyulabs`,
  or promote the previous READY deployment in the Vercel dashboard.
- Apex routing (after activation): `/usr/bin/python3 /opt/commandry/deploy/commandry-rolling.py rollback`
  restores the retained release and its routing, per
  [rolling releases](../../../deploy/rolling-releases.md); reinstalling the prior
  controls removes the public route split.
